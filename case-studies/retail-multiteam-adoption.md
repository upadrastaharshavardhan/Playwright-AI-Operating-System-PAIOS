# Case Study: Retail Multi-Team Adoption

## Context
A retail organization with 12 quality engineering teams across 40 microservices adopted PAIOS incrementally, starting at Autonomy Level 1 ([Autonomous Testing whitepaper](../whitepapers/Autonomous%20Testing.md)) per team.

## Outcome
Shared [Failure Memory](../docs/05-memory-engine/failure-memory.md) surfaced cross-team root-cause matches that previously required manual escalation between teams, reducing mean-time-to-diagnosis for recurring issues.
